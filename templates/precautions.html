🛡️ Medicine Overdose Precautions

💊 Check Medicine Dosage

Always read the label carefully and follow the prescribed dosage to avoid accidental overdose.

📅 Track Your Intake

Keep a record of all medicines you take daily, including supplements and painkillers.

🚫 Avoid Mixing Drugs

Never mix medications without consulting a doctor, especially painkillers and other prescriptions.

🧠 Check Health Conditions

Inform your doctor about any chronic conditions such as liver, kidney, heart, or diabetes.

⚠️ Watch for Symptoms

Seek immediate help if you experience dizziness, nausea, or unusual fatigue after taking medicines.

📞 Emergency Contacts

Keep emergency numbers handy and consult a healthcare professional when unsure about dosages.
